2. Personal Data & Document Privacy

We do not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide personal details (such as your name, phone number, or address) to use InstaPDF.

  • Your Photos & Images: Any images you select from your device or capture using your camera remain strictly in your device's local memory during arrangement, rotation, filtering, and PDF conversion.
  • Your Generated PDFs: Converted PDF files are saved directly to your device's internal storage or your local Downloads/InstaPDF folder when you choose to download or share them.
  • Zero Cloud Uploads: AKM_Studio does not operate any cloud storage server that receives your documents, watermarks, or filenames.

3. Device Permissions We Use

To provide core functionality, InstaPDF requests only the minimum required Android system capabilities:

  • Photo Selection (Android System Photo Picker): We use Android's official zero-permission Photo Picker (PickMultipleVisualMedia), which grants the app temporary read access strictly to the specific photos you choose to convert into a PDF, without requiring broad access to your entire photo library.
  • Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA): Requested only when you tap the "Open Camera" button so you can capture photos of physical documents, receipts, or notes to convert into PDF pages. You can deny or revoke this permission at any time in your Android System Settings.
  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used only for lightweight tactile feedback during user interface interactions.
  • Network Access (INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE - When Advertising Is Active): Used solely by third-party advertising SDKs (such as Google AdMob) to request and display banner advertisements and check network connectivity.

When advertisements are active in the app, Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable and device-level information in accordance with Google's privacy policies, including:

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because AKM_Studio does not collect or store your personal files on external servers, all temporary cache files created during camera capture or PDF generation reside solely on your device. You can delete your generated PDFs at any time using your device's File Manager, or clear all temporary app data by going to Android Settings > Apps > InstaPDF > Storage > Clear Data / Clear Cache, or by uninstalling the app.

6. Data Security

We prioritize the security of your documents by designing the conversion engine to run entirely offline on your device. File sharing (when you tap "Share / Save to Device") uses Android's secure FileProvider content URI architecture with temporary read permissions granted only to the target app you explicitly select.

7. Children's Privacy

InstaPDF is a general-audience productivity tool and is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

8. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Depending on your jurisdiction (including the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and California), you have rights regarding transparency, data access, and opting out of targeted advertising. Since AKM_Studio does not store personal user profiles on its own servers, you maintain direct physical control over all files on your device and can manage ad-related identifiers directly through your Android OS settings.
